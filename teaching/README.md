# Teaching College Algebra

Use the [chapter guide](../chapters/README.md) and start by asking for a valid and invalid studio session input. The proposed arc moves from exposed restrictions (Chapters 1–2) to modeling and discrete choices (Chapters 3–5). No institutional syllabus or verified course delivery is claimed. See the [queue Blueprint](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/ai-plus-one-paper/blob/2dc37042e9556e51d10c0787e0072c43ece3ef60/book-queue/editorial-blueprints/21-college-algebra-ai-one.md) for the curricular scope.

## Use the reservation case

Hand out [Part A](../practice/worksheet.md#part-a-write-the-initial-recommendation) before showing the Chapter 5 graph. Collect initial plots and predictions, then release the 8-dollar observation in [Part B](../practice/worksheet.md#part-b-release-the-evidence). Ask learners to defend a modeling choice, not just improve a residual. Pair review can identify missed boundary cases; optional AI critiques may be used only once the initial attempt is recorded. Both routes require hand substitution or an independently rebuilt table and disclosure of which critique changed the work.

| Criterion | Evidence for full credit | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Domain | Contextual interval, join and rational exclusion are correct and justified | 3 |
| Algebra and graph | Both models trace to observations, with slope units and labeled axes | 3 |
| Independent evidence | Held-out prediction and residual verified without refitting | 2 |
| Decision and revision | Capacity proof and feasible mix before and after the change | 3 |
| Record | First attempt, revision and verification trace preserved | 1 |

For a fresh transfer prompt give at least five extended reservations and only 41 setup minutes. The maximum is 18 because $2(s+e)+5\le41$, attained at $(13,5)$. At 6 dollars the 18 modeled requests then fit exactly; no extrapolation or refit is required. Score the independent recollection of the constraint, not agreement with a model-generated answer.

## Optional AI critique and disclosure

Allow only a critique of one recorded assumption or one proposed edge case. Do not outsource the first graph, derivation, feasibility proof or independent transfer. Keep student records and private course work out of external tools. In the submitted record, name the tool if used, quote the proposed critique, show an independent recomputation, and explain what you accepted or rejected. Mark “no AI” if you used the peer or paper route. Apply the same rubric in either case; format alone does not earn points. Release the fresh transfer without the answer during the independent check, and have the learner defend one inequality aloud or in a short supervised written note.
