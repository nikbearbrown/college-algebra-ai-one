# College Algebra (AI Plus One)

## About the book

This compact rough draft teaches students entering college algebra or preparing for applied modeling how to move among equations, graphs and decisions. You will carry each equation's allowed inputs through a solution, check a model against a new observation, and turn capacity constraints into a defensible price recommendation. Bring fluency with fractions, signed numbers, plotting points and one-variable equations; [Prealgebra](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/prealgebra-ai-one) is the bridge if those need practice. These five chapters cover the compact Blueprint, not the predecessors’ full trigonometry, analytic geometry and counting units. The draft is not an expert-reviewed edition.

## Chapter guide

**Part I: Respect the domain**

1. [Functions as mappings](chapters/01-functions-as-mappings.md): translate a studio's fee table and trace the allowed inputs of a composition.
2. [Equations and domains](chapters/02-equations-and-domains.md): solve equations without losing roots through division or adding them through squaring.

**Part II: Model change and constraints**

3. [Exponentials and logarithms](chapters/03-exponentials-and-logarithms.md): fit a repeated factor and convert a continuous threshold into a weekly decision.
4. [Systems and feasible regions](chapters/04-systems-and-feasible-regions.md): draw intersecting limits and prove an integer capacity bound.
5. [Building and challenging models](chapters/05-building-and-challenging-models.md): fit piecewise demand, test a withheld point and expose an extrapolation failure.

## How to teach or study it

Start with input contracts, then carry those contracts into solving and modeling. Readers do short unaided checks within each chapter and finish with a [reservation worksheet](practice/worksheet.md): hand in a graph, residual table, capacity proof and revised decision. The [teaching guide](teaching/README.md) includes a rubric and transfer prompt. A calculator, a peer or the optional [offline checks](examples/README.md) can supply independent evidence; no AI account is required. The [editorial Blueprint](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/ai-plus-one-paper/blob/2dc37042e9556e51d10c0787e0072c43ece3ef60/book-queue/editorial-blueprints/21-college-algebra-ai-one.md) is a proposed scope, not a syllabus or a sign-off.
